Rules in short

  • Every ball, both sides secretly pick a number from 0 to 6.
  • If your number matches the opponent's number → the batter is out.
  • If it doesn't match → the batter scores their own chosen number as runs.
  • Win the toss, choose to bat or bowl first.
  • After innings 1, the target for innings 2 is that score + 1.
  • Whoever scores more (or chases the target first) wins the match.
